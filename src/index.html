Our Training Programs

Cataloguing Standards & Metadata

Learn MARC, RDA, Dublin Core and other essential metadata standards for effective resource description.

  • Outcomes: Master metadata principles, apply standards.
  • Duration: 8 Weeks
  • Skill Level: Beginner - Intermediate

Digital Libraries & Repository Management

Gain expertise in managing digital collections, institutional repositories, and digital preservation.

  • Outcomes: Design digital libraries, manage repositories.
  • Duration: 10 Weeks
  • Skill Level: Intermediate

Information Organization & Access

Understand principles of information organization, indexing, and retrieval systems for optimal access.

  • Outcomes: Develop taxonomies, improve search.
  • Duration: 6 Weeks
  • Skill Level: Beginner
